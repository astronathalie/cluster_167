About this tool
Cluster stars are drawn from synthetic photometry generated using a Kroupa initial mass function sampled along an isochrone matching the cluster's age and metallicity, with binarity (~40% of stars combined with companions, producing the visible double sequence) and magnitude-dependent scatter. Isochrones come from a small in-house grid (not PARSEC or MIST), so turnoff positions and giant-branch shapes are approximations rather than publication-grade tracks.
The turnoff mass MTO drops with age roughly as t−0.4; reading it off a cluster's CMD dates the cluster.
